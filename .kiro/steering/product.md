# Product: Colaby

Colaby is a private team collaboration web app for a small, invite-only team. The owner invites team members via email. The team consists of:

- **Owner** — manages the app and invites members
- **Supervisor** — a direct report manager who oversees the team's work
- **Business Analysts** — team members who attend meetings, report tasks, and contribute to projects

---

## Core Features

### 1. Access Control
- Only the owner can invite members by email
- All users must sign in to access the app
- After sign-in, users land on a dashboard where they can view or update any of the sections below

### 2. Meeting Minutes
- Record minutes for team meetings
- Each entry includes:
  - Meeting title
  - Date
  - Summary
  - Full minutes / notes
- Supports the "split and conquer" workflow where team members attend different meetings and log their own minutes

### 3. Weekly Reports
- Team members submit weekly reports to the supervisor
- Reports summarize work done during the week
- Supervisor can view all reports from team members

### 4. Task Tracker
- Log assigned tasks with their current status
- Statuses track progress (e.g., Not Started, In Progress, Completed, Blocked)
- Visible to all team members and the supervisor

### 5. Project Tracker
- Log ongoing projects and their current state
- Similar status tracking as tasks
- Provides a high-level view of team deliverables

---

## User Experience

- After signing in, users see a dashboard with access to all sections
- Any team member can view existing records
- Any team member can create or update records as needed
- The supervisor has visibility into all reports, tasks, and projects from the team

---

## Audience

Small, closed team (~5–10 people). Not a public product. Prioritize simplicity and clarity over scale.
